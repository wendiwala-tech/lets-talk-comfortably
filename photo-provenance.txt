These are source photographs downloaded from the photographers’ Pexels and Unsplash pages, not AI-generated images. Production files have only been proportionally resized and encoded as WebP; no people, expressions, or backgrounds have been generated or changed. Campus locations are not represented as Berkeley. The sexual-health cover uses a quiet table scene without people.
