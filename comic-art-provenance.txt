Comic reaction sprite sheets — Let’s Talk Comfortably
Created: 2026-11-19
Tool: built-in image_gen.imagegen (not CLI/API fallback)
Purpose: AI-generated editorial comic frames for fictional adult conversation practice; not real students, participants, testimonials, or research results.

The original website illustrations are preserved unchanged. New sheets derive the adult character designs from those existing AI-generated illustrations. They are strictly two columns by three rows, six square frames in row-major order:
0 gentle nervous opening
1 attentive listening
2 puzzled / uncertain
3 calm clarification
4 respectful pause / space
5 friendly acknowledgment / relief

Production assets: dist/assets/comic-pair-mixed.webp, comic-pair-women.webp, comic-pair-men.webp.
Source PNGs retained at ../comic-art-source/ outside the repository. WebP files are format-compressed versions only; no cropping, redrawing, compositing, or programmatic background removal was performed.
All sheets: 1024 x 1536 pixels, RGBA. Transparency is generated alpha. No embedded text, labels, or dialogue; accessible labels and actual story dialogue are rendered by the website.
